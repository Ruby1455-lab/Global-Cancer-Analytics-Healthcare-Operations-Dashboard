# Global-Cancer-Analytics-Healthcare-Operations-Dashboard

Project 2: Global Cancer Analytics & Healthcare Operations Dashboard Business Problem:
Built a comprehensive multi-page healthcare analytics dashboard to track patient outcomes, diagnoses, treatment costs, and hospital performance across top medical institutions (Cleveland Clinic, Mayo Clinic, Johns Hopkins, MD Anderson, Sloan Kettering)

Tools Used: Power BI Desktop, Advanced Data Modeling, DAX, KPI Cards, Multi-page Report Slicers

Key Insights:

* Established a baseline 61.56% overall patient survival rate across 450 tracked patients.  
* Uncovered that survival rates remain consistent across hospitals (55%–63%), proving that doctor-level performance is a more critical operational lens than facility choice.  
* Flagged a declining trend in monthly follow-up visit frequencies, highlighting potential patient retention or care coordination gaps.
